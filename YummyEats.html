Baingan Bharta
Ingredients:
  • 2 large eggplants
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
Instructions:
  1. Roast the eggplants on an open flame until the skin is charred and the flesh is soft.
  2. Peel the skin off the eggplants and mash the flesh.
  3. Heat oil in a pan, add onions and green chilies, and sauté until onions are golden brown.
  4. Add tomatoes, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt. Cook until tomatoes are soft.
  5. Add the mashed eggplant and mix well. Cook for a few minutes.
  6. Garnish with cilantro and serve hot.

Chole Bhature
Ingredients:
  • 1 cup chickpeas
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1/2 inch ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
Instructions:
  1. Soak chickpeas overnight and pressure cook until soft.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds, onions, green chilies, and ginger. Sauté until onions are golden brown.
  3. Add tomato puree, turmeric powder, red chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Cook until oil separates.
  4. Add cooked chickpeas and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. For bhature, mix flour, yogurt, salt, and water to make a soft dough. Let it rest for an hour.
  6. Roll out the dough into small discs and deep fry until golden brown.

Burger Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb (450g) ground beef
  • 1/2 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup bread crumbs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles (optional, for toppings)
  • Condiments of your choice (ketchup, mustard, mayo, etc.)

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine ground beef, onion, egg, bread crumbs, milk, salt, and pepper. Mix until well combined.
  2. Divide the mixture into 4 equal parts and form into patties.
  3. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the patties for 4-5 minutes per side, or until cooked to your desired level of doneness.
  4. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster oven.
  5. Assemble the burgers with the patties on the bottom bun, topped with lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and condiments of your choice. Top with the other half of the bun and serve.
Risotto Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 cups Arborio rice
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Optional: mushrooms, peas, or other vegetables

Instructions:

  1. In a saucepan, heat the broth over low heat.
  2. In a separate large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until translucent.
  3. Add the rice to the skillet and stir to coat with the oil. Cook for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Gradually add the wine to the skillet, stirring constantly, until the liquid is absorbed.
  5. Begin adding the warm broth to the skillet, one ladleful at a time, stirring constantly and allowing each addition to be absorbed before adding the next.
  6. Continue adding broth and stirring until the rice is creamy and cooked to al dente, about 20-25 minutes.
  7. Remove from heat and stir in the Parmesan cheese, butter, salt, and pepper.
  8. Serve hot, optionally topped with additional Parmesan cheese.
Croissant Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 1/4 teaspoons (1 packet) active dry yeast
  • 1/2 cup (120ml) warm water (110°F/45°C)
  • 3/4 cup (180ml) warm milk (110°F/45°C)
  • 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 cups (500g) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 cup (2 sticks or 230g) unsalted butter, cold

Instructions:

  1. In a small bowl, dissolve yeast in warm water and let stand until foamy, about 5 minutes.
  2. In a large bowl, combine warm milk, sugar, and salt. Stir in the yeast mixture. Gradually mix in flour until dough holds together.
  3. Turn dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead until smooth and elastic, about 8 minutes. Place in a greased bowl, turning once to grease the top. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, about 1 hour.
  4. Punch dough down, cover, and refrigerate for 8 hours or overnight.
  5. Roll out dough into a 12-inch square. Cut butter into 1/2-inch slices and arrange over dough. Fold dough over butter and seal edges. Roll out dough into a 20x10-inch rectangle. Fold into thirds, starting with a short side. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  6. Repeat rolling and folding two more times. Roll out dough to 1/4-inch thickness. Cut into triangles and roll up, starting from the wide end. Place on greased baking sheets and let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
  7. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Bake croissants in preheated oven until golden brown, about 15-20 minutes. Cool on wire racks.
Cheesecake Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 cups (150g) graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 24 oz (680g) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
  • 1 cup (240ml) sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C). Grease a 9-inch springform pan.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs, sugar, and melted butter. Press mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan.
  3. In a large bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth. Add sour cream and vanilla extract, and beat until combined. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
  4. Pour cream cheese mixture over crust in the springform pan. Smooth the top with a spatula.
  5. Bake in preheated oven for 55-60 minutes, or until the center is almost set. Turn off the oven and let cheesecake cool in the oven for 1 hour.
  6. Remove cheesecake from oven and let cool completely. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight, before serving.
Crispy Calamari Rings Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb (450g) calamari rings
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • Oil for frying

Instructions:

  1. Place the calamari rings in a bowl and pour the buttermilk over them. Let them soak for 30 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, salt, pepper, and paprika.
  3. Heat the oil in a large pot or deep fryer to 350°F (175°C).
  4. Remove the calamari rings from the buttermilk and dredge them in the flour mixture, shaking off any excess.
  5. Fry the calamari rings in batches for 2-3 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy.
  6. Remove the calamari rings from the oil and place them on a paper towel-lined plate to drain excess oil.
  7. Serve the crispy calamari rings hot with your favorite dipping sauce.

Veggie Pad Thai Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 200g rice noodles
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • 3 green onions, chopped
  • 1/4 cup peanuts, chopped
  • Lime wedges, for serving
  • For the sauce:
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon tamarind paste
  • 1 teaspoon sriracha sauce (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Cook rice noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, lime juice, tamarind paste, and sriracha sauce to make the sauce.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.
  4. Add sliced bell pepper and julienned carrot to the pan. Cook for about 3-4 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp.
  5. Add the cooked rice noodles, bean sprouts, chopped green onions, and the prepared sauce to the pan. Toss everything together and cook for another 2-3 minutes.
  6. Serve Veggie Pad Thai hot, garnished with chopped peanuts and lime wedges.
Vegan Lentil Soup Recipe

Vegan Banana Bread Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup coconut oil, melted
  • 1/2 cup maple syrup
  • 1/2 cup almond milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, combine mashed bananas, coconut oil, maple syrup, almond milk, and vanilla extract.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
  4. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients and stir until just combined. Fold in chopped walnuts, if using.
  5. Pour batter into prepared loaf pan and bake for 50-60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

Roasted Poblano Vegan Enchiladas Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 4 large poblano peppers
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 8-10 corn tortillas
  • 1 can enchilada sauce
  • 1 cup vegan cheese, shredded
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  2. Place poblano peppers on the baking sheet and roast in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, turning halfway through, until the skins are blistered and charred.
  3. Remove the peppers from the oven and place them in a bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and let them steam for 10 minutes.
  4. Peel the skins off the peppers, remove the seeds, and chop them.
  5. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and garlic and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  6. Add chopped poblanos, black beans, diced tomatoes, cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Cook for another 5 minutes, then remove from heat.
  7. Spread a thin layer of enchilada sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  8. Fill each tortilla with a spoonful of the black bean mixture and a sprinkle of vegan cheese. Roll up and place seam side down in the baking dish.
  9. Pour the remaining enchilada sauce over the top of the enchiladas and sprinkle with the remaining vegan cheese.
  10. Bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  11. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro.
Vegan Chipotle Lentil Rolled Tacos Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup cooked lentils
  • 1 chipotle pepper in adobo sauce, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 8-10 corn tortillas
  • 1/4 cup vegan sour cream
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1/4 cup chopped red onion
  • Lime wedges, for serving

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix together the cooked lentils, chipotle pepper, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Warm the corn tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat until pliable, about 30 seconds per side.
  3. Divide the lentil mixture among the tortillas, spreading it out in a line down the center of each tortilla.
  4. Roll up the tortillas and place them seam side down on a plate.
  5. Serve the rolled tacos topped with vegan sour cream, cilantro, red onion, and lime wedges.
